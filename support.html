Common questions

Does Snap Croco delete photos right away?
No. Swiping only marks a photo. Photos move to Recently Deleted only after you finish a review, tap “Move to Recently Deleted” and confirm the iOS prompt. You can restore them from Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days.

Why does a photo take a moment to appear?
If you use iCloud Photos with “Optimize iPhone Storage”, older photos may need to download. Snap Croco moves those photos to the end of your review and downloads them in the background, so you can keep swiping.

Snap Croco shows no photos or asks for access.
Open iOS Settings → Apps → Snap Croco → Photos and choose Full Access, or Limited Access with the photos you want to review.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything happens on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
