Snap Croco Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Snap Croco is made by Nick Roesler. In short: your photos stay on your iPhone, the app has no accounts, ads or analytics, and nothing about you is sent to us.

Your photos

Snap Croco uses the Photos access you grant to show your photos and videos so you can review them. Everything happens on your iPhone. Snap Croco never uploads your photos or sends them to the developer or anyone else.

If you choose Limited Access, the app only sees the photos you selected. You can change access at any time in iOS Settings.

Marking a photo for deletion only saves your choice. Photos are moved to Recently Deleted only after you confirm at the end of a review and approve the iOS prompt. You can restore them from Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days.

What is stored on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, Snap Croco saves your review choices, review progress, your preferences and reminder settings on your iPhone. A summary of your progress is shared with the Snap Croco widget on the same device.

This information never leaves your device through Snap Croco. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, as with any app. Deleting Snap Croco removes it from your iPhone.

Apple services

If your library uses iCloud Photos, iOS downloads full-quality photos from iCloud when you review them. That download is handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone as local notifications. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

What we collect

Nothing. Snap Croco has no account, no server, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. We don’t collect, sell or share personal information.

If you email us or send TestFlight feedback, we only use what you send to reply and improve the app.

Children

Snap Croco does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page with a new effective date.
