Your photos

Snap Croco reads your library only so you can review it. It works with whatever access you give it, including Limited Access, and you can change that any time in Settings.

Marking a photo only remembers your choice. Nothing is removed until you confirm at the end of a review and approve the iOS prompt. Removed photos go to Recently Deleted, where you can get them back for 30 days.

What the app saves

To pick up where you left off, the app keeps your review choices, progress, settings and reminder times on your iPhone. The home screen widget reads a small progress summary from the same device.

This data is not sent anywhere. It can be part of your own iCloud or computer backup, like any app's data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Apple services

If you use iCloud Photos, iOS downloads full-size originals from iCloud when you open them. Apple handles that under its own privacy policy. Snap Croco never sees your Apple Account.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. No push server is involved.

What I collect

Nothing. Snap Croco has no servers, so there is nothing to collect, sell or share.

If you email me or send TestFlight feedback, I only use what you send to reply and fix things, and I delete it when it's no longer needed.

Your rights

Because I hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, export or delete on my side. Laws like the GDPR and CCPA still give you those rights, and you can email me any time if you have a question.

Children

Snap Croco doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page with a new date at the top.
